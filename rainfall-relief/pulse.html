Where to get data

Any gridded series with one raster per year (or period) works. Some places to look:

  • PRISM: US precipitation and temperature, 1895–now (built in)
  • CHIRPS: global rainfall, 1981–now, ~5 km GeoTIFFs
  • TerraClimate: global climate and water balance, ~4 km
  • ERA5-Land: global reanalysis, 1950–now (export as GeoTIFF)
  • NOAA nClimGrid: US temperature and precipitation, 5 km
  • NASA GPM IMERG: global satellite rainfall

Upload your own

One raster per year (GeoTIFF, BIL, ASC or IMG; zips are fine) with the year in each file name, e.g. precip_2005.tif, all covering the same area. Or one multi-band GeoTIFF, one band per period. Any projection is reprojected for you. Max 2 GB per file.
