Scope. Live trees (STATUSCD = 1) on each state's latest FIA evaluation. Diameter and height are the raw field measurements; biomass is aboveground + belowground dry weight; volume is net merchantable cubic-foot volume. None of these are expanded to a per-acre total here — the "weighted" view corrects the shape of the distribution for FIA's sampling design, it is not a population total.

Why two views. FIA measures trees under 5 inches diameter on a smaller microplot and trees 5 inches and up on the full subplot. A raw tree count is pulled from a bigger search area once a tree crosses that threshold, which shows up as an artificial dip-then-spike right at 5 inches in the raw sample. Weighting each tree by trees-per-acre (TPA × the microplot/subplot adjustment factor) removes that artifact and shows the smooth, steadily declining size distribution of a natural, uneven-aged forest.
